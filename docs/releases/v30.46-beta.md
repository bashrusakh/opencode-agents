# v30.46 beta

## Changed

- Extends the canonical root evidence rule from generic test/setup validity to proposition-scoped evidence fidelity: material preconditions, qualifiers, scope, and observable/oracle semantics must survive into verification artifacts.
- Separates structural, behavioral, and evidentiary coverage so complete changed-file/diff review cannot be reported as complete acceptance-proof coverage without discriminating evidence.
- Requires failing as well as passing artifacts to establish validity for the governing proposition before they are used as product defect/pass evidence; otherwise the result remains a verification/test defect, environment-dependent evidence, or evidence gap.
- Adds proportional role-specific applications for acceptance-critical planning, regression-fixture construction, tester causality checks, reviewer claim-to-evidence reasoning, design-vs-implementation adequacy at semantic checkpoint, orchestrator proof-obligation handoff, and session-evaluator evidence-laundering diagnostics.
- Updates the Semantic Design Manual with evidence-fidelity and coverage methodology.
