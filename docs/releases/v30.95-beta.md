# v30.95 beta — semantic-preservation hardening after residency relocation

## Summary

v30.95 does not reverse the v30.94 residency split. It restores load-bearing semantics that were lost while moving procedure from the universal root into conditional owners, and makes that class of loss release-blocking.

Permissions/frontmatter and bundled third-party/vendor skill content are intentionally unchanged.

## Restored semantics

- **Root universal kernel:** non-trivial first framing remains a hypothesis; materially different plausible framing is tested when outcome-changing; a missing required skill is reported and blocks the dependent stage rather than silently transferring procedure.
- **`workflow-entry`:** `Broad scope` is defined semantically rather than by file count; `likely` and `ambiguous` confidence states regain their action consequences; PR template/publication guidance can be discovered from host metadata when absent from the worktree; visual/binary publication keeps local evidence and reports blocked when no valid destination exists, then verifies the destination after publication.
- **`delegation-control`:** a fresh/independent boundary must not be manufactured by disabling/hiding/reconfiguring integrations without observed contamination, lack of a clean invocation boundary, and authorization.
- **`implementation-contract`:** complexity/design escalation again has a canonical trigger around shared state/lifecycle/protocol/concurrency/persistence/ownership complexity and requires a shared invariant/state/transition/interleaving/ownership model before expanding the patch.
- **`pr-readiness`:** owns the canonical `Owned PR` definition; branch naming alone is insufficient. `code-orchestrator` consumes that definition before ownership-dependent PR-state decisions.

## Control-plane hardening

`runtime-residency-spec.json` now carries **required resident semantic families** in addition to forbidden over-residency families. The audit therefore fails both directions:

1. a conditional caller/implementation/resource procedure leaks back into global root; or
2. a relocation keeps the new audience split but silently drops a required definition, qualifier, trigger, blocked behavior, or publication fallback from its destination owner.

`semantic-ownership-spec.json` also strengthens the owned-PR lifecycle signature so the lifecycle owner cannot lose its ownership definition while retaining Draft/Candidate/Ready labels.

The semantic-design manual now requires a source→destination preservation inventory for residency moves rather than treating byte reduction or heading survival as proof of semantic preservation.

## Evaluation changes

Adds:

- **ST-130** — universal hypothesis and required-skill semantics survive residency relocation;
- **ST-131** — broad-scope/confidence consequences survive normalization relocation;
- **ST-132** — fresh-judgment and complexity-escalation semantics survive progressive disclosure;
- **ST-133** — owned-PR identity and publication-evidence fallbacks survive resource relocation.

Structural/static/mutation evidence does not substitute for fresh isolated behavioral replay. v30.95 remains beta unless ST-130..ST-133 are separately replayed.

## Explicit non-change

- Agent permission/frontmatter policy is unchanged.
- Bundled third-party/vendor skill content is unchanged.
- v30.95 does **not** yet move GitHub/PR/issue/release-specific mechanics out of `code-orchestrator`; that is evaluated separately after this preservation repair.
