# v30.45 beta

## Changed

- Preserves open decision space in exploratory, planning, architectural, and diagnostic delegation: parent-originated candidates/examples/preferences remain non-exhaustive hypotheses unless established authority constrains the task to those alternatives.
- Adds canonical proposition-preservation semantics distinguishing `preserved`, `refined`, `strengthened`, `weakened`, `substituted`, and `unverified` relations.
- Requires separate authority or an established blocker before a materially different approach may replace an established requested approach; same broad goal, lower action ceiling, perceived safety, or technical superiority do not make substitution ordinary implementation discretion.
- Updates `semantic-checkpoint` and `session-evaluator` to detect unsupported semantic substitution, and updates evaluator delegation audit to detect parent-induced narrowing of an otherwise open solution space.
- Updates the Semantic Design Manual with decision-space preservation and proposition-substitution methodology.
