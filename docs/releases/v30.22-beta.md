# OpenCode Agent Pack v30.22 beta

## Scope

Authority-provenance semantic fix built on v30.21 beta.

## Changes

- Root §2.2.1 now states that derived reasoning does not create authority. Material requirements must trace to normalized user intent, an applicable project-local rule, or a necessary consequence of one of them.
- Review findings, inferred invariants, tests, implementation choices, comments, assignments, and prior agent decisions remain evidence until that authority is established.
- Necessary technical consequences may proceed without escalation when the established outcome cannot be met without them and they do not strengthen the required behavior, widen authorized scope, create a new observable contract, or authorize another gated action.
- `code-orchestrator` re-checks authority before widening an implementation boundary or dispatching downstream fixes from implementation/review findings.
- `reviewer` may inspect sibling manifestations of an established invariant, but a broader invariant inferred during review is reported as a hypothesis/design gap until authoritative acceptance establishes it.
- Root §7.2 ties outward ownership-boundary movement to the established outcome and makes explicit that moving outward does not strengthen that outcome.

## Prompt-design basis

The wording was cross-checked against OpenAI's current model prompting guidance:

<https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/guides/latest-model>

The change keeps one canonical root rule, uses short role-specific triggers, and avoids mandatory provenance reports or multi-option reasoning rituals.
