# v30.56 beta

## Changes

- Adds a general delegation-composition invariant: a specialist assignment specializes the selected role contract; it does not replace, waive, or redefine role-owned obligations, stop conditions, independence requirements, or verdict semantics.
- Explicitly permits narrower diagnostic assignments while requiring their result to remain partial; a partial specialist result cannot satisfy a broader stage.
- `session-evaluator` now audits the missing composition edge `assignment → selected role contract` and detects laundering of a partial result into a full-stage verdict.
- The semantic transition suite expands from 27 to 28 scenarios with ST-28 covering invocation/role-contract composition.
- No reviewer-specific duplicate rule is added; root delegation policy remains the canonical owner and role prompts continue to own their intrinsic methodology.

## Validation status

Static structural checks and mutation probes are recorded in the companion validation report. Fresh isolated replay of ST-01…ST-28 remains separate behavioral evidence and is not claimed by this release.
