# v31.11 beta — Evidence-based interim communication across roles

The always-loaded `AGENTS.md` §2.3 now requires concise intermediate updates for **substantial ongoing work** at meaningful evidence or coverage milestones while material work remains, addressing the active recipient (user for primary roles; caller for delegated roles) **when the channel permits**. Updates say what has been established, what remains unresolved, and what meaningful investigation/action comes next. They do not narrate routine tool calls, repeat unchanged progress, or imply a premature final verdict.

This extends the existing requirement to disclose material **changes in decisions**; it does not replace or replicate it. The `workflow-control` user-facing Startup/Update format and `code-orchestrator` workflow-state checkpoints remain conditionally owned and unchanged. Delegated specialists do not acquire user-facing workflow ownership, mandatory fixed-frequency messages or prescribed progress fields.

The design manual's communication guidance was aligned with delegated recipient/channel boundaries. All agent bodies, frontmatter and skills are unchanged; no new ST, mandatory stage, permission or role topology. Static release gates do not prove that a real model or host UI emits interim messages; behavioral replay is not claimed.
