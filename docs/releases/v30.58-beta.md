# v30.58 beta

## Changes

- The existing reviewer-owned authoritative-domain preservation rule is generalized from value/parser/representation transforms to candidate behavior or accepted-value restrictions imposed through a narrower domain.
- `reviewer` now explicitly treats packaging/build/release matrices and other non-authoritative operational surfaces as evidence about their own coverage, not as authority to narrow an accepted/supported behavior domain unless authoritative policy establishes that relation.
- Root task-semantic authority, qualifier preservation, conditional fallback, exact-candidate evidence freshness, and post-mutation read-back semantics are unchanged. No platform-specific rule or second semantic owner is introduced.
- ST-24 remains the generalized authoritative-domain regression; ST-29 and ST-30 remain eval-only regressions of existing exact-state/read-back rules. The semantic transition suite stays at 30 scenarios.
- Static policy regressions now require the generalized operational-surface reviewer wording so a future prompt edit cannot silently regress runtime coverage back to representation-only cases.

## Validation status

Static structural regressions, mutation probes for the generalized reviewer guard, and package checks pass. Fresh isolated behavioral replay of ST-01…ST-30 remains separate evidence and is not claimed by this release.
